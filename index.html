How good is your gut for physics?

Five quick tests, two rounds each. Guess where water settles, where a wheel stops, where a light hangs, how long things take and where a ball rolls. No rulers, no clocks.

10 rounds · about 3 min

  1. Level

    A glass of water tips over. Draw where the water sits.

  2. Spin

    A wheel slows down, then gets covered. Guess where the red slice stops.

  3. Lamp

    Posts cast shadows from a hidden lamp. Find the lamp.

  4. Hold

    Hold the button for as long as an everyday moment lasts.

  5. Bank

    A pool ball rolls, bounces off the sides and vanishes. Guess where it ends up.
